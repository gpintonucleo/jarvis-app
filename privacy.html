Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

JARVIS is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on his own computer. There are no other users.

What data it uses

With the owner's consent, JARVIS uses Google APIs to read his Gmail messages and settings, draft and send emails he approves, and read and create events in his Google Calendar.

How the data is handled

  • The data is processed on the owner's own PC, only to do what he asks.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Nothing is sent on his behalf without his explicit approval.
  • The access token is stored only on his computer; he can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
